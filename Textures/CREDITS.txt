Popperpig Raid Call — guide screenshots
=======================================

The .tga files in this folder are NOT original artwork. They are the
positioning diagrams from two community raid guides, converted to a format the
WoW client can load, and shipped here with credit.

    Mount Hyjal
        winterchill.tga   anetheron.tga   kazrogal.tga
        azgalor.tga       archimonde.tga

        From Jurdi's Mount Hyjal Cheat Sheet.
        https://www.twitch.tv/jurdijd
        https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v7CgKFX45iw

    Black Temple
        najentus.tga    supremus.tga    akama.tga      teron.tga
        bloodboil.tga   reliquary.tga   shahraz.tga    council.tga
        illidan.tga     illidan_p2.tga  illidan_p3.tga
        illidan_p4.tga  illidan_p5.tga

        From cosmophile's Black Temple guide.

The strategies and the screenshots are theirs. This addon only puts them on
screen at the moment they are needed. If either author would rather these were
not redistributed, remove this folder and the addon keeps working — the drawn
diagrams in Data/Positions.lua are the default view and do not depend on any
of these files.

Regenerate with:

    python3 scripts/build-textures.py --hyjal <dir> --bt <dir>

512x512, uncompressed 24-bit TGA. Power-of-two because the client requires it;
uncompressed because WoW's support for RLE-encoded TGA is not dependable, and a
texture that silently fails to load is worse than a larger file.
